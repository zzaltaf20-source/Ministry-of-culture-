04 · चार अध्याय · Four chapters

Heritage, told in four materials

Stone that outlives empires, leaf that outlives libraries, sound that outlives its players, and movement that outlives its choreographers.

Editorial storytelling · concept

CHAPTER I

Stone

Temples, stupas and forts — India's longest memory is carved, not written. Conservation keeps the carve legible.

CHAPTER II

Leaf

Palm-leaf and birch-bark manuscripts carry grammar, medicine and poetry — fragile archives now being digitised leaf by leaf.

CHAPTER III

Sound

Raga is architecture in time. Archival recordings keep the masters in the room for students not yet born.

CHAPTER IV

Movement

Dance holds scripture in the body — mudra, tala and stance passed guru to shishya, unbroken.
